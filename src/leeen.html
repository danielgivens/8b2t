A little lean. A little fun.

Leeen is a playful iPhone app with visuals and sounds that react to movement. Tilt your phone to explore music, moving objects, and other motion-reactive experiences.

For iPhone with iOS 18 or later. Made by {{DEVELOPER}}.

FAQs

How do I get started?

Open Library, tap an experience, and gently tilt your phone. Swipe within experiences that have multiple versions to try another one. For a guided introduction, open Help in the app. You can turn the help panel back on in Settings → Show help.

How do I reset the center or clear lean records?

In a full-screen experience, press and hold the scene to reset its center, then confirm. To clear your lean records, press and hold the record area and confirm. Settings → Show records controls whether records are visible.

Can I use two experiences together?

Press and hold an experience in Library or Favorites, then tap a second experience to open them together.

Why does Leeen ask for location?

With your permission, Leeen uses speed and compass direction to change some visuals and sounds. Your route is not recorded. Location is optional: if you decline, motion experiences still work and speed-reactive effects use a preset speed. Change permission in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Leeen.

Does Leeen work offline?

The motion experiences work without an internet connection. Optional tips, this website, and the helpdesk need internet access. A photo stored in iCloud may need to download before you can import it.

Where does my photo go?

If you choose a photo for the Danglers photo frame, Leeen saves a resized copy in the app on your phone. It does not upload the photo to us or give us access to your photo library. See the privacy policy for storage and deletion details.

What are the tips in the Info panel?

Tips are optional, one-time purchases processed by Apple. They do not unlock features, start a subscription, or purchase a physical item. All experiences are available without tipping. Tips can be given more than once and are not restorable feature purchases. For a billing issue, follow Apple’s refund instructions; Apple determines eligibility.

Why is the app using more battery?

Motion sensing, location, sound, and animated visuals can use extra power. Turn off Animate thumbnails in Settings to reduce activity in Library and Favorites, and close an experience when you are finished.

Can I rely on Leeen’s readings?

Leeen is for entertainment. Its readings are estimates, not precision measurements or safety guidance. Set it up while stopped, keep your attention on your surroundings, and do not interact with the app while operating a vehicle.

Privacy policy

Last updated: {{POLICY_DATE_LABEL}}

This policy covers the Leeen iPhone app and its support website, provided by {{DEVELOPER}} (“we,” “us”).

Data used on your device

Leeen processes the following information on your phone to provide its features. The app does not send this information to us:

  • Motion: device orientation, acceleration, and related sensor readings make the experiences react to movement. Calibration and peak lean records may be saved locally; the app does not keep a history of raw motion readings.
  • Location and direction: with your permission, iOS location and compass readings provide speed and heading for visuals and audio while you use the app. Leeen does not save your route or location history.
  • Photos: if you select an image through Apple’s photo picker, Leeen saves a resized copy locally for the Danglers photo frame. It accesses the image you select, not your entire library.
  • Preferences: settings, favorites, recently used experiences, and saved experience controls are stored locally to remember your choices.

Accounts, advertising, and tracking

Leeen does not require an account. The app has no advertising, third-party analytics SDKs, or tracking across other companies’ apps and websites. We do not sell or share app data for targeted advertising.

Optional purchases and Apple services

Apple processes optional tips. Leeen uses Apple’s transaction information on your device to verify and acknowledge a purchase. We do not receive your payment card details. Apple separately handles App Store transactions, device backups, and any diagnostic sharing you enable under Apple’s privacy policy.

Support requests

If you contact us, we receive the contact details, message, and attachments you choose to submit. We use them to respond, resolve issues, and handle privacy requests. Please send only what is needed for your request. Support correspondence is kept only as long as needed for those purposes or applicable legal obligations.

This website

This website does not add analytics, advertising cookies, or tracking scripts. It is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub processes technical information, including visitors’ IP addresses, to provide and secure its service. See GitHub’s privacy statement for its practices and retention. External services you choose to visit have their own privacy policies.

Retention, deletion, and your choices

Live sensor readings are processed for the current experience. Saved preferences, calibration, records, and the selected photo remain in the app until replaced, reset where a control is available, or removed by deleting the app.

Clear lean records by holding the record area in a full-screen experience and confirming. Choosing a new frame photo replaces the app’s previous copy. To remove all locally saved Leeen data, use Delete App in iPhone Settings; Offload App keeps app data. Deleting Leeen does not delete the original image from your photo library.

Your device’s backup settings may include local app data. Manage or delete backups separately through Apple’s settings; restoring a backup may restore that data. Apple retains purchase records under its own policies.

You can withdraw location permission in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Leeen. You can stop using a feature or delete the app to stop its on-device processing.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or obtain a copy of personal information we hold, restrict or object to its use, or complain to your local data protection authority. Use the help & contact section to request help. We cannot retrieve data stored only on your device.

Children’s privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through a support request, contact us so we can address it and delete it as appropriate.

Changes and contact

We will update this page and its date when our practices change. For questions about this policy or your information, visit help & contact.
